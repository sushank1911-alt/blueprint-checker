Abstract: The Examination Question Paper Blueprint Validation System is an automated academic software tool developed in C that audits, verifies, and analyzes semester examination question papers against prescribed institutional blueprints. In autonomous engineering colleges such as Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering & Technology, question papers must adhere strictly to assessment guidelines established under Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy. The system verifies that questions and marks are evenly distributed across course units, Course Outcomes (CO1 to CO5), and cognitive levels (L1 Remember to L6 Create).
